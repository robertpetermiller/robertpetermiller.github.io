Pen-and-ink plates. Boats on Biscayne Bay and the Channel coast, one plate for every class ever sailed at the Olympic Games, and a beginning in colour.

Series I · Boats

Egret, Presto, Romilly and Farfarer — four shoal-draught boats, drawn where they belong and a few places they don't: Key Biscayne, Dover, the French coast, the Bahamas.

Series II · The Olympic classes

Every class sailed at the Games since 1900, in order of first appearance. Each plate is drawn from a single period reference; where no photograph survives, after the class drawing. Crews as photographed, faces turned away.

Series III · Colour

A beach, drawn in ten coloured pencils — each pencil seeing only the part of the picture that belongs to it, the ten drawings laid one over another.

About

The plates are drawn in a house style of clean line and hatched tone, at 17 × 24 inches, for print on heavy matte paper. Robert Peter Miller lives on Key Biscayne, Florida, and sails on Biscayne Bay.
